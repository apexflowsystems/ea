Install guide

After you receive SMElite by email, install it in MetaTrader. Adjust inputs for your broker’s digits.

MT4 / MT5 steps

  1. Copy the EA into MQL4/Experts or MQL5/Experts
  2. Restart MetaTrader or refresh Navigator
  3. Attach to the chart (e.g. XAUUSD M1/M5)
  4. Allow Algo Trading / AutoTrading
  5. Match lot and risk inputs to your account size

Broker digits tip

  • Some brokers quote gold with an extra decimal (e.g. 3 vs 2). PIP-related inputs may need an extra 0.
  • Example: if InpMinProfitClose = 2 on 2-digit style pricing, scale with lot size (e.g. 0.01 lot → about $2; 0.1 lot → about $20) and add a zero where your broker’s digit format requires it.
  • Always forward-test on demo with your exact broker first.
